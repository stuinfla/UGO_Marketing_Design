A small pill for countries, fields of study and category legends. Pulls from the eight campaign hues.

```jsx
<Tag tone="lime">Cambodia</Tag>
<Tag tone="pink" solid>Pharmacy</Tag>
<Tag tone="darkTeal" dot>Bangladesh</Tag>
```

`tone`: pink · lightTeal · darkTeal · lime · orange · cornflower · darkGreen · royalBlue. `dot` renders the map-legend style (colored dot + plain label); `solid` fills the pill.
