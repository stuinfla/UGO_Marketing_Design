U-GO UNIVERSITY BRAND KIT FOR CLAUDE — SETUP (one time, about 2 minutes)

You need: a Claude account (claude.ai website or the Claude desktop app). Do NOT unzip this file.

1. Open Claude. Click your name/initials (bottom left) > Settings > Capabilities.
   Turn ON "Code execution and file creation".
2. In the left sidebar go to Customize > Skills.
3. Click "+" / "Upload skill" and choose ugo-university-design.zip.
4. Make sure "ugo-university-design" is switched ON.

That's it. From now on, in ANY chat, just start your request with "U-GO:"

   U-GO: make a 6-slide PowerPoint for donors about our Nepal programme.
   U-GO: build a one-page landing page for our 2027 scholarship appeal.
   U-GO: turn this report (attached) into a branded PDF one-pager.
   U-GO: write 3 LinkedIn posts announcing 50 new scholars, plus a square image for each.

Claude will use the real U-GO fonts, colours, logo and watercolour silhouettes and give you a finished file to download.

Tip: attach real photos of scholars and say "use these photos" for the best results.
If the brand fonts aren't installed on your computer, PowerPoint will show similar standard fonts. Websites and PDFs use the real fonts.
